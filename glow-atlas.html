How to take a photo that measures well
  • Face the camera straight on, eyes level, neutral expression, mouth closed.
  • Hold the phone at eye level and about an arm's length away, or use the back camera with a timer. Close-up selfies widen the nose and narrow the face.
  • Even, soft light from the front (a window in front of you). No filters, no beauty mode.
  • Pull hair back so the hairline, ears and jaw corners are visible.
  • Photos stay in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded.
